About Us

Frontend Simplified is a (kinda) free, open-source curriculum that teaches you how to become a frontend developer. We believe that anyone can learn to code, and we want to make it as easy as possible for you to get started. Regardless of your background, we will teach you everything you need to know to become a frontend developer. Our curriculum is designed to be easy to follow, and we provide plenty of resources to help you along the way.
